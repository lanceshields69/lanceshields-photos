Night is falling on Mission St. Shadows grow in the corners. Stores are shuttered and the sidewalks have a quiet settle over them. Some people go indoors, while others come outside. Song of Mission St is a look at the pre-nocturnal urban when day turns to night. Rough around the edges like we are but faint music plays around every corner.
