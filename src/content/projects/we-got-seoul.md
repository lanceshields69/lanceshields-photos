One week wandering the streets of Seoul, Korea. Striking color culture contrasted with bright skies and simple styles. So many faces young and old, studying the details of their world. I relished ducking down narrow alleys packed with food and supplies of all kinds. Is this the Asia I know? Or is it changing too fast to really know. I left Seoul feeling like I left part of myself behind.
