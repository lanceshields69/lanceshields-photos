During a work sabbatical I traveled to the UK, Spain, and Denmark. In this project, I explored the essence of isolated individuals amidst the urban landscapes of Manchester, Barcelona, and Copenhagen. The project explores the interplay between solitude and urbanity, revealing moments of quiet contemplation and introspection amidst the noise of city life. Each photograph serves as a single fragment of a larger narrative, seeking the beauty of fleeting moments of solitary figures. The photos are a glimpse into the transient nature of human existence within environments that I am a stranger myself.
